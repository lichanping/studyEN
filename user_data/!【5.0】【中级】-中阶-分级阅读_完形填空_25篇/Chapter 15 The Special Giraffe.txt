There was a giraffe called Allan. He lived in a zoo with his mother, dad and other giraffe friends. As Allan grew, he found his neck was much shorter than the others. He began to get worried. But his mother told him that he was special. She said it did not matter how short or long his neck was.
Allan tried not to think about his short neck. But as his friends grew taller and taller, his neck stayed short. Sometimes the other giraffes laughed at him, but his mother told him not to worry . She said that he should be happy with himself the way he was. He tried, but he couldn’t even play in some giraffe sports. He was just too short.
One day, one of the zoo workers came and took Allan away. Allan was scared. Would he have to leave the zoo just because he didn’t have a long neck? The zoo worker calmed Allan down and gave him some food. He felt better. Then the zoo worker led Allan into another area of the zoo. There was a small door. Allan was the only giraffe that could fit through it.
Soon Allan’s parents came to see him. “I told you that you were special, ” his mother said. “The zoo has made a place just for you because so many people want to see how special you are! ” She was right. Visitors pointed to him and screamed excitedly . They looked happy to see something so special at the zoo.

【重点短语搭配】
1、laugh at
含义：嘲笑
用法：动词短语，后接名词或代词；常用于劝诫或描述负面行为。
例句：Don't laugh at others when they make mistakes.（翻译：别人犯错时不要嘲笑他们。）
2、not to worry
含义：不要担心
用法：固定口语表达，用于安慰他人；相当于Don't worry。
例句：Not to worry, everything will be fine.（翻译：别担心，一切都会好起来的。）
3、calm down
含义：平静下来；使冷静
用法：动词短语，可及物或不及物；常用于安抚情绪。
例句：The teacher asked the student to calm down and take a deep breath.（翻译：老师让学生冷静下来，深呼吸。）
4、fit through
含义：挤过；穿过（狭小空间）
用法：动词短语，后接名词；描述穿过窄小通道。
例句：The cat is small enough to fit through the hole in the wall.（翻译：这只猫很小，能穿过墙上的洞。）
5、point to
含义：指向
用法：动词短语，后接名词；常用于指示方向或物体。
例句：The little boy pointed to the bird in the tree.（翻译：小男孩指向树上的鸟。）
6、be happy with
含义：对……感到满意/开心
用法：形容词短语，后接名词或代词；表达满足感。
例句：She is happy with her new school and friends.（翻译：她对她的新学校和朋友感到满意。）
